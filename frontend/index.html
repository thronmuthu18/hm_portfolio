Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about services and workflows

What services do you provide?

I offer responsive website development, landing page creation, business websites, portfolio development, end-to-end full stack development, frontend translations, website redesigns, maintenance and support checks, troubleshooting bug fixes, and basic SEO configurations.

Do you build responsive websites?

Yes, all websites I build utilize fluid typography and container queries to adjust layout structures automatically on mobile, tablet, and wide screen monitor viewports.

Which technologies do you use?

I primarily work with Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB Atlas (Mongoose), HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript. For web applications hosting, I utilize GitHub, Netlify, and Render.

How can clients contact you?

You can send an email directly to sudalaimuthusudalai754@gmail.com, call me at +916382539787, or fill out the secure contact form below. I typically respond within 24 hours.

How long does a typical project take?

A standard landing page or simple business portfolio takes 1 to 2 weeks. Custom full stack web applications requiring database integrations and secure backend APIs typically require 3 to 4 weeks depending on the complexity of features requested.

Do you provide website maintenance?

Yes, I offer monthly maintenance agreements that cover package version updates, server uptime check monitoring, design tweaks, security patches, and troubleshooting bug fixes.
